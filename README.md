CONTENT WARNING: body horror, photosensitivity

Game Name: Too Odd to Handle

Group: Dead Center Studios 

Group Members: Michael kopczynski, Ryan Holdstock, Jason Acosta, Anna Benbow

Game Description: Too Odd to Handle is a horror comedy dating sim game that takes place in a building called The Studio. It is a reality tv dating show. The cast is made up of 4 creatures. The first is The Host, the host of the “show.” He informs the player on what to do, speaks to the audience, and interacting with him is part of the core gameplay loop. Milton is a sentient worm, Kyle is an alpha-maxxing wendigo, and Sabina Carpeter is a skinwalker. There are thousands of lines of branching dialogue, 3 different dates, and several unique endings the player can discover. The first date is a speed date with all 3 members of the cast, the second date is a romantic dinner where you choose who you want to go with. Halfway through, another cast member interrupts. You also choose who to go out with for the final date, which changes depending on who you pick. Kyle’s final date is a truck driving sim to Atlantic City, Milton’s final date is doing chores for him around his hovel, and Sabina’s final date is a rhythm game. 

Game Instructions: Launch the game and press any key to start. Follow the tutorial.

Game Walkthrough: Press any key to start. Follow the tutorial. After you are summoned, the game show begins. After The Host stops talking, walk up to each character and press E to interact with them. After their dialogue is done, walk back to The Host and interact with them, then pick yes. Progress through the speed date, choosing whatever options you see fit. Then, once you are in the next intermission phase, speak to each character again after The Host stops talking using the same mechanics as last time. Give any character the red rose. Progress through the second date by choosing any options you’d like. Take the golden rose when prompted to complete the game quickly, or choose the red rose again and take all 3 characters on a date, progressing through as normal. Then, once you are in the next intermission phase, speak to each character again after The Host stops talking using the same mechanics as last time. Give one of the characters the golden rose. If you choose Milton, speak to him in his hovel, then go to the broom cabinet, press e to pick up the broom, and press e to sweep all the books and the cockroach. Then speak to him again, and go to the kitchen area and press e to open the sandwich menu. Any sandwich works, but his affection will differ based on how well you made it. Next, speak to him again to give him the sandwich and receive your next task. Go over to his computer and click the X on any popups that show up. Speak to him one more time to end the date. If you choose Sabina, complete the rhythm game by pressing the arrow keys when the corresponding arrow reaches its corresponding arrow. Better performance is more affection. If you choose Kyle, dodge the obstacles using the arrow keys and make it to Atlantic City. After the third date is done, speak to the characters and ask them to leave with you. If they like you, they will. If not, they won’t. If no one likes you, you lose. If you leave with someone, you “win.”

Cheat Codes: 
Delete - restart the game
Ctrl - Go to next room in the game project up until the win room
Alt - Change game phase (a enumeration defined in the create function of game controller, used for the logic with the dialogue system. Dialogue interactions only appear when you're in the correct game phase, so be mindful not to accidentally hit it when trying to play through the dialogue!)
Tab - When in date 1, cycle through each character's speed dates

Graduate Requirements: Paths were implemented in the Milton date 3 with the cockroach. It follows a path and moves around the room. Particles were implemented in Kyle date 3, emitting smoke from the truck. A shader was implemented in Kyle date 3, flashing the car when hit. Views are used throughout the game. Object inheritance is used for interaction with objects with E. The cockroach in Milton’s room uses AI by running away when the player is near.
